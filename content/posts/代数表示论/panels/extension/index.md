<h3 class="ar-subhead">猜想陈述</h3>
<p><strong>Extension 猜想</strong>（Y. Han, 2013）：设 $\Lambda$ 为 Artin 代数，$S$ 为单模。若 $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}_{\Lambda}(S,S)\neq 0$，则对所有 $i\ge 1$ 都有</p>

$$ \operatorname{Ext}^{i}_{\Lambda}(S,S)\neq 0, $$

<p>即"一次自扩张 ⇒ 处处自扩张"。</p>

<ul class="ar-timeline">
  <li><span class="ar-year">2013</span>Y. Han 宣称证明（arXiv:1309.0304, <i>A proof of extension conjecture</i>）。</li>
  <li><span class="ar-year">⛔ 撤回</span>该证明<strong>已被作者撤回</strong>（"due to a crucial but wrong claim"），故该猜想目前<strong>仍视为开放</strong>。</li>
  <li><span class="ar-year">现状</span>单项式代数等特殊类已知成立；一般情形开放。</li>
</ul>
