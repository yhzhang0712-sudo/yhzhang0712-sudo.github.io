<h3 class="ar-subhead">Problem 1: Approximability of $\mathbf{D}(A)$ for Dg Algebras with $H^{i}(A)=0$ for $i\gg 0$</h3>
<p><strong>Question:</strong> Let $A$ be a dg algebra with $H^{i}(A)=0$ for $i\gg 0$. Is $\mathbf{D}(A)$ approximable, or weakly approximable, with respect to the natural $t$-structure? Either prove it, or give a counterexample: an $A$ with $H^{i}(A)=0$ for $i\gg 0$ whose $\mathbf{D}(A)$ is neither approximable nor weakly approximable.</p>
