<h3 class="ar-subhead">猜想陈述</h3>
<p><strong>Happel 的"分数 Calabi–Yau"猜想</strong>：代数闭域上每个有限维代数都（导出等价于）一个<strong>扭分数 Calabi–Yau 代数</strong>——即存在 $m>0$ 使 Serre 函子的某次幂等价于一个平移 $\Sigma^{n}$（"Serre 函子最终周期性"）：</p>

$$ S_{\Lambda}^{m}\ \simeq\ \Sigma^{n} \qquad (\exists\, m>0,\ n\in\mathbb{Z}) . $$

<h3 class="ar-subhead">研究现状</h3>
<ul class="ar-timeline">
  <li><span class="ar-year">2001</span>Happel 提出该猜想。</li>
  <li><span class="ar-year">2000s</span>对遗传、piecewise hereditary（Happel）、monomial（Dugas, <i>Periodicity and orbits of derived categories</i>, 2012）、radical-square-zero 等类已证。</li>
  <li><span class="ar-year">2026</span>A. Chan, <i>Fractionally Calabi–Yau algebras and cluster tilting</i>（arXiv:2604.19582）把有限全局维数的扭 fCY 代数刻画为 bCY 范畴中 cluster-tilting 对象的稳定自同态代数。</li>
  <li><span class="ar-year">现状</span>一般情形<strong>仍开放</strong>（🔴）。</li>
</ul>
