<h3 class="ar-subhead">猜想陈述</h3>
<p><strong>无环猜想（NLC）</strong>：设 $\Lambda$ 为有限维代数，$S$ 为单模。若 $S$ 有有限投射维数，则 $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}_{\Lambda}(S,S)=0$（即在普通箭图中该顶点无环）。</p>
<p><strong>强无环猜想（SNLC）</strong>：若单模 $S$ 满足 $\operatorname{Ext}^{1}_{\Lambda}(S,S)\ne 0$，则 $\operatorname{pd}_{\Lambda}S=\infty$。</p>

<h3 class="ar-subhead">研究现状</h3>
<ul class="ar-timeline">
  <li><span class="ar-year">2011</span>SNLC 对<strong>代数闭域上有限维代数</strong>已被 K. Igusa 完全证明（<i>The strong no loop conjecture</i>, Adv. Math. <b>228</b> (2011), arXiv:1103.5361）。</li>
  <li><span class="ar-year">现状</span>对<strong>一般 Artin 代数（任意域/完全域之外）</strong>，NLC/SNLC 的完全形式仍常被列为开放问题。</li>
</ul>
